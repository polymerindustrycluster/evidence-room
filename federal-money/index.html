Polymer obligations at place of performance, FY2019–2026

Federal money has been landing on polymer work here every year for eight years.

PIC's funding map shows the awards PIC helped win. This shows the background rate — ordinary federal contracting in chemical and plastics manufacturing across the same twelve counties. It is the context that makes the Tech Hub award legible as either large or ordinary, and PIC has never put the two side by side. Two limits bound everything below, and both are structural rather than incidental. The industry view filters to chemical and plastics manufacturing codes only, so university and research awards are invisible to it by construction — a university files under 61xxxx. And place of performance is a reported field on the award, not an observation of where the work happened.

Obligation — money the government has legally committed to pay. It is a promise on the books, not cash already spent, and it is the figure federal spending data reports. Place of performance is where the work is done, which is not always where the company is headquartered.
