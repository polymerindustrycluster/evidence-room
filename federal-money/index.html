Is the Tech Hub award big? · USAspending obligations at place of performance, FY2019–2026

Routine federal contracts bring the region’s polymer industry about $35 million a year.

The EDA Tech Hub award is $51.0 million. Ordinary federal contracting in chemical and plastics manufacturing across the same twelve counties has run at $34.9 million a year since 2019, so the award is worth about a year and a half of money the region was already getting. In fiscal 2019 the routine flow alone came to $51.6 million, more than the whole award. PIC has never published the two numbers side by side, and it is the comparison every funder conversation about the award already assumes.

Obligation: money the government has legally committed to pay. It is a promise on the books, not cash already spent, and it is the figure federal spending data reports.

$51.0M of Tech Hub money is about a year and a half of what already arrives

Federal obligations to polymer industry codes at place of performance, by fiscal year, in 2025 dollars. FY2026 is a partial year.

Where the work is recorded. Place of performance is a reported field on the award, not an observation of where the work happened. A centrally administered contract, or a prime performing through a subaward, can put the county code somewhere other than the activity. An obligation is also not an outlay: it is money committed, which may be spent across several years or de-obligated.
