A stable workforce, or a flat one?

The region recorded 111,529 hires to end up with 168 more jobs.

Every employment chart PIC publishes shows a stock: how many jobs exist. Underneath, plastics and rubber manufacturing in these twelve counties starts about a tenth of itself and ends about another tenth, every quarter. These are job events, not people: a Summit-to-Stark move counts twice.

The rate

Turnover has risen since 2012 and has not come back down

Churn rate is the average of hires and separations over the jobs that exist: the share of the workforce that moved in or out that quarter. The pandemic spike is visible, and so is the fact that the line settles above where it started.

The four-quarter average ends at 9.6%, about 1.1 points above 2012

Which side moved

The extra turnover is people leaving

The rate above is the average of two flows, and only one of them moved. Between 2012 and the last four published quarters the separation rate rose about 1.9 points while the hire rate rose about 0.2. In 2012 hiring ran 0.9 points ahead of leaving; on a four-quarter average leaving has run ahead in every quarter since 2023Q1.

Where the turnover concentrates

A high rate on a small base is a different problem from a high rate on a large one, so this reads on two axes at once: the rate a county runs, and the number of jobs it runs it on. Twelve counties are twelve readings of the same measure by the same method, and they are the only cross-sectional comparison on this page.

On a shop floor

What 10.8% a quarter looks like at a 150-person plant

Averaged over fourteen years, the cluster moves 10.8% of its jobs in a quarter. At a 150-person molder that is about 16 people starting and 16 leaving every quarter, roughly five of each in a month, and about 65 replacement hires to make in a year. The plant is illustrative: it applies a cluster average to a single site, and no single plant runs at the average.

plastics or rubber plant, any of the twelve counties

Flat is what fast looks like from a distance.

Between 2012Q1 and 2025Q3 the cluster gained 168 net jobs across 222,890 hire and separation events, a flow about 1,327 times larger than the number it is scored on. Net employment is a legitimate outcome measure, and funders are entitled to score it. The point is that a program changing how people move through this industry can do real work without moving that number, and that since 2023Q1 the movement has run toward the door.
