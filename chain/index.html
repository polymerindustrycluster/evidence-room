Supply against demand

Applications and register records concentrate at different stages

The register includes 235 converters and fabricators, compared with 12 raw-material and feedstock companies. PIC’s 2026 funding round received 20 applications naming conversion and 26 naming raw materials and feedstock. Both sources use the same stage taxonomy, but they cover different populations: company records with qualifying addresses in fourteen Northeast Ohio counties, and all 59 applications wherever the applicant sits; 38 are in Ohio. These counts do not measure capacity, competition or market demand. Applications reflect one funding round, including its eligibility rules and applicants’ awareness of it.
