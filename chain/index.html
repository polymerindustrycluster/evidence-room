Applications cluster where cataloged companies are fewest

PIC’s intake form and the vault use compatible value-chain taxonomies, so the companies on record and the projects people want funded can sit on one spine. They point opposite ways: converters have 12 cataloged companies for every 2026 application; raw materials and feedstock has one for every two. Read that as coverage, not as competition. It is applications measured against how many companies the vault has classified, not against capacity, headcount or output. Twelve raw-materials companies may be very large plants; 246 converters may be small shops. And the two sides come from different footprints (intake is PIC-12, the vault is NEO-14), so the ratio is indicative and is the one place on this site where the two geographies touch. It is on the page for that reason.
