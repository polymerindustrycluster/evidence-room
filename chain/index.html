The Polymer Industry Cluster’s register carries 710 company records with qualifying addresses in fourteen Northeast Ohio counties. It is a record inventory, not a census of regional companies or plants. Seventy-five previously included records are held out and named below. The molecule end means raw materials and feedstock. Register records against funding applications

Applications and register records concentrate at different stages

The register includes 235 converters and fabricators, compared with 12 raw-material and feedstock companies. PIC’s 2026 funding round received 20 applications naming conversion and 26 naming raw materials and feedstock. Both sources use the same stage taxonomy, but they cover different populations: company records with qualifying addresses in fourteen Northeast Ohio counties, and all 59 applications wherever the applicant sits; 38 are in Ohio. Of the 710 regional records, 566 carry a classified role and 490 carry one of the stage roles drawn here; the other 76 carry only distribution, logistics, construction, engineering or energy roles, which no stage on this chart covers. These counts do not measure capacity, competition or market demand. Applications reflect one funding round, including its eligibility rules and applicants’ awareness of it.

The evidence underneath

How the register is built

The company records stay private. The rules that admit them are published here, so a reader can check what the register can and cannot contain.

Sources

Company notes in the GAC-PIC vault, PIC’s internal company register, extracted on 14 August 2026. Each company’s description is reproduced as the company wrote it. Census County Business Patterns 2023 supplies separate establishment context, and PIC’s 2026 intake export supplies the application counts.

Which records count

A record counts when its county is one of the fourteen PIC-12+2 counties, the twelve PIC-12 counties plus Columbiana and Tuscarawas, and its state is Ohio, OH or blank. A county name alone is not enough: records that matched a county name in another state, sat outside the region or had no address are held out and named in the data. A record’s stage comes from the role tags in its note, mapped to six chain stages and four supporting services. A record with no role tag stays in the count and out of the chain.

How often it changes

There is no fixed schedule. The register changes when PIC re-extracts the vault. The 14 August 2026 extract was re-checked against the county rule on 11 September 2026 without being re-extracted. Each change to a published figure is dated in the corrections log.

What stays private

The vault notes are not published. This page shows each company’s name, website where one is recorded, roles and own description; the held-out names are disclosed so the exclusions can be checked.
