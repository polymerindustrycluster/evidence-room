Why here

The capacity is a century old: Goodyear in 1898, PVC in 1926, a national science center by 1991

Before there was a cluster to measure, there was a place. PIC’s register can prove 19 events that changed the region’s capacity and 13 scientific firsts, each against a named public source, and every mark below opens its source. A discovery is a paper or a patent, never a product, and none of this is a cluster outcome; it is the reason the cluster is here. The register’s withholding rules are in the methods.

The company that moved

In September 2024 BioVerde CEO Dave Witte announced the company would move the bulk of its operations to Ohio, citing the grant. Its $11.1M bio‑based butadiene award began that December, the largest of the five company R&D awards.

The school that started early

Sixteen days after the designation, NovationSi and RD Abbott ran a polymer workshop at Barberton High School. Fourteen months later Barberton launched a K‑12 Polymer Pathway curriculum, backed by its community foundation.

The promises

18 events are still only scheduled, and the R&D clocks run to 2029

Ten of the scheduled dates come due by the end of 2026, most of them first-year milestones on the federal awards. The other eight land between 2027 and 2029: the pilot facility’s readiness target and seven award or project end dates. The five company R&D awards that began in December 2024 run to end dates between November 2027 and November 2029. Until then the public R&D record is progress milestones, and a milestone is not a product.
