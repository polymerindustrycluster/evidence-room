Before the record

Why here: 1898 to 2012

Before there was a cluster to record, there was a place. These are the events PIC’s register can prove changed the region’s capacity, and the scientific firsts it can prove happened here — a discovery is a paper or a patent, never a product. None of it is a cluster outcome; it is the reason the cluster is here. Every mark opens its source. Rows the register labels only claimed — where a date or a figure is recorded two ways — are held back rather than shown with a caveat, and the wartime synthetic-rubber program is one of them.
