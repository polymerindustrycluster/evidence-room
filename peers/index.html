Where does Ohio rank?

Ohio led the country in plastics and rubber jobs in 2024.

Ohio had 54,846 private plastics and rubber jobs in 2024. The record discloses every state plus Puerto Rico; DC is absent. Metro rankings are incomplete: the Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS) withholds employment in 227 of 382 metro areas.

By John Swanson, Polymer Industry Cluster desk · Analysis and graphics by Claude (Anthropic), updated with Codex (OpenAI) · Data: the federal Quarterly Census of Employment and Wages (QCEW), annual averages, 2015–2024

The clean claim

The top 20 states, and Ohio is first with no asterisk

States are the one geography BLS publishes in full: 51 of 51 disclosed, none withheld.

The narrow claim

Akron and Cleveland among the metros we can see

Measure the same 155 metros BLS discloses two ways. Seventeen of them carry 4,000 or more plastics and rubber jobs. In 37 of them, the industry fills at least twice as much of the local job base as it does of the country’s. Seven metros are in both counts. Those seven are large and concentrated at once, and Akron is one of them. Among the 155 disclosed metros, Akron ranks sixth on plastics and rubber jobs and eighth on concentration. Cleveland is the other half of the cluster’s story, bigger on jobs and much less concentrated, which is what a large diversified metro looks like in this industry.

Who else is up there

Akron sits alongside six industrial peers

Six other metros are large and concentrated at once. Elkhart, Indiana has recreational-vehicle manufacturing. Hickory, North Carolina makes furniture and fiber-optic cable. Erie, Pennsylvania has mold-making shops. Greenville, South Carolina holds Michelin’s North American headquarters. (BMW’s assembly plant is next door in Spartanburg County, a separate metro area outside this row.) Scranton and Greensboro fill out the seven. Those industry descriptions are context from separate county data and company sources. Akron earns its place in that group the way the other six do, on its job count and its concentration. Its 6,910 jobs sit across 117 establishments, an average of about 59 people a site. That average is taken across sites of every size, so it describes the group and not any one plant.

Industry context: BLS 2024 annual county files for Elkhart, Catawba (Hickory) and Erie; Corning’s March 2023 campus announcement; Michelin North America’s company profile; BMW’s plant page. Plant locations do not establish metro-wide production shares.
