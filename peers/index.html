Where Northeast Ohio actually sits, nationally

Ohio is first in the country in plastics and rubber employment.

In plastics and rubber products manufacturing, private ownership, 2024 — and that clause has to travel with the sentence, because it is a narrower industry than “polymers” and a single year of a series the Bureau of Labor Statistics revises. Within it: first in absolute jobs, ahead of California, Texas and Michigan, with all 51 state-level geographies disclosed. No suppression asterisk. Several scope asterisks. The metro claim is smaller than PIC has been saying, and this page says why.

Suppressed — the bureau withholds a figure when too few employers exist for it to be published without identifying one of them. The jobs are real; the number is simply not disclosed, which is not the same as zero. An establishment is one physical site, not one company. Concentration here means the region's share of an industry measured against the country's: 1.0 is exactly the national share.
