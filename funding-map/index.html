Public awards to the polymer cluster, as of August 2026

Three public awards, $106 million, and no two move the money the same way

Governments awarded $85.3 million to the Greater Akron polymer cluster, and partners and the state promised $21.0 million more beside it. Of the awarded money, $79.2 million already names who receives it. This is every dollar, every program, and every organization it reaches.

By the Polymer Industry Cluster desk · Analysis and graphics Claude (Anthropic), directed and reviewed by J. Swanson · Data signed federal Notices of Award and the executed Ohio grant agreement · Figures as of 13 August 2026

    The state’s grant lands in one Akron hub and splits five ways; the biggest slice, $18.52M, is a building.

  3. Machine 3 · Good Jobs Challenge (APEX) · $3.08M federal

    One workforce award, held by the Greater Akron Chamber, which signs for it as grantee, funds regional training aimed at 500 enrollments and 320 placements.

    The local match commitment beside it, $3.37M, is larger than the award itself. Trace it on the map

What a line item buys

Two award labels carry more than they say

The map gives each award a few words. Two of those labels describe work that a few words cannot hold.

Flexsys · $10.09M · EDA R&D

Replacing the tire additive behind the coho salmon die-offs

Nearly every tire on the road carries 6PPD, an anti-degradant that keeps rubber from cracking. Its breakdown product, 6PPD-quinone, is the chemical a 2021 Science paper identified as killing coho salmon in urban Pacific Northwest streams. Flexsys’s award funds development of a safer alternative in Akron.

See the award

Full Circle Technologies · $4.70M · EDA R&D

Un-making a tire is the hard part

A tire is mostly vulcanized rubber, built never to melt back down, so recycling one into new products is far harder than recycling a bottle. Full Circle’s award funds end-of-life tire recycling, the only recycling project among the seven direct federal awards.
