What a line item buys

Two of the terse award labels above carry more story than they let on.

Flexsys · $10.09M · EDA R&D

Replacing the tire additive behind the coho salmon die-offs

Nearly every tire on the road carries 6PPD, an anti-degradant that keeps rubber from cracking. Its breakdown product, 6PPD-quinone, is the chemical a 2021 Science paper identified as killing coho salmon in urban Pacific Northwest streams. Flexsys’s award funds development of a safer alternative in Akron.

See the award

Full Circle Technologies · $4.70M · EDA R&D

Un-making a tire is the hard part

A tire is mostly vulcanized rubber, built never to melt back down, so recycling one into new products is far harder than recycling a bottle. Full Circle’s award funds end-of-life tire recycling, the only recycling project among the seven direct federal awards.
