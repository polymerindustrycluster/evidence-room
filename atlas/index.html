Every institution in the federal education record, 1991–2023

The country teaches polymer science in 147 places.

Between 1991 and 2023, 147 American colleges and universities reported a polymer degree or certificate to the federal government. Three of them account for more than a third of all the degrees and certificates in that record: the University of Massachusetts Lowell, Ferris State University in Big Rapids, Michigan, and the University of Akron. The 147 is a floor rather than a full count, because the record sees only the programs colleges chose to report under a polymer code.

By John Swanson, Polymer Industry Cluster desk · Analysis and graphics by Claude (Anthropic) · Data IPEDS completions by 6-digit CIP, IPEDS institutional directory · August 2026
