US polymer education records, 1991–2023

147 teaching records since 1991; 41 recorded polymer awards in 2023.

Three of the 147 account for more than a third of all the degrees and certificates in that record: the University of Massachusetts Lowell, Ferris State University in Big Rapids, Michigan, and the University of Akron. Each record follows a federal institution identifier; one school can have more than one. Teaching reported outside polymer codes is missing. The 41 is one year of filings, not a roll-call of open schools: an institution absent from it may still be teaching the subject, under another code or none.

By John Swanson, Polymer Industry Cluster desk · Analysis and graphics by Claude (Anthropic), updated with Codex (OpenAI) · Data IPEDS completions by 6-digit CIP, IPEDS institutional directory · Updated September 2026
