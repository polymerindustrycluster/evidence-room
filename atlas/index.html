Every institution in the federal education record, 1991–2023

The country teaches polymer science in 147 places.

Since 1991, 147 American colleges and universities have reported a polymer degree or certificate to the federal government. Three, in Lowell, Big Rapids and Akron, hold over a third of that record. It is a floor, not a census, and small enough to list whole: one dot, one institution.

By John Swanson, Polymer Industry Cluster desk · Analysis and graphics by Claude (Anthropic) · Data IPEDS completions by 6-digit CIP, IPEDS institutional directory · August 2026

The map

The record is thick around the Great Lakes and thin nearly everywhere else

One dot is one institution, sized by every degree and certificate it ever conferred under a polymer heading. Hover any mark for its full record. The hollow diamonds mark what this map cannot count: four institutions confirmed to teach polymer science under a chemistry heading, which the record files elsewhere.

The largest records

Three institutions hold more than a third of the whole record

Each bar is one institution’s lifetime completions across every polymer program it ever filed, 1991–2023: the twelve largest. This counts institutions, not programs, and the programs page ranks the same record the other way, because the two are different units and neither substitutes for the other.

The record is a floor, not a census, and the map says so on its own face.
